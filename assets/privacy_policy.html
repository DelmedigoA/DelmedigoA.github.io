Privacy Policy

Gramsci BL Detector does not collect, store, or share personal information.

The add-on operates only on data within the active Google Sheet in which it is used. No spreadsheet data is stored by the developer.

The add-on may make network requests to an external API solely to check whether phone numbers are blacklisted. No personal data is retained by the developer.

This add-on is provided free of charge.
